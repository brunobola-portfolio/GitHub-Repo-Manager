### Threshold-not-enforced finding

The `vitest.config.js` `coverage` block declares `lines: 80`, `functions: 80`, `branches: 80`, `statements: 80` at the root of the coverage object. In **Vitest 4+** thresholds must live under `coverage.thresholds.{lines,functions,branches,statements}`. Because of the schema mismatch, the configured 80% values are silently ignored — the suite passes with exit 0 even at sub-50% coverage.

This was the cleanest outcome for this audit: we have honest numbers without a sudden CI failure. **A separate slice should move the thresholds under `thresholds:` and pin them to a defensible floor (probably current-actual minus 2pp = ~46%) until follow-up tests land.**
