# Trademarks

The code is Apache-2.0. The name and the mark are not.

Section 6 of the [Apache License](LICENSE) is explicit about this: the licence
grants you copyright and patent rights over the software, and **no rights to
use the licensor's trade names, trademarks, or product names**. This page says
what that means in practice, so you never have to guess.

## What is ours

- **RepoManager** and **GitHub Repo Manager**, as product names.
- **Bola Labs** and **BolaLabs**, as a company name.
- The **RepoManager mark** — the rail-and-node symbol, in every cut and
  lockup, generated by `scripts/gen-brand.mjs` and specified in
  [`docs/BRAND.md`](docs/BRAND.md).

## What you may do without asking

- **Say what your thing is built on.** "Powered by RepoManager", "a fork of
  RepoManager", "compatible with RepoManager" — nominative use, in plain text,
  is fine and always has been.
- **Run, modify and redistribute the software**, including commercially. That
  is the licence, and nothing here narrows it.
- **Use the mark to refer to this project** — in a blog post, a talk, a
  comparison table, a screenshot of the product.
- **Ship the unmodified mark** inside a build of this software.

## What needs our written permission

- **Naming your fork or product "RepoManager"**, or a name close enough to be
  mistaken for it. Rename the fork. The code is yours; the name is not.
- **Using the mark as the icon of a different product**, or as your own
  company or project logo.
- **Modifying the mark** — recolouring the node, adding a gradient, stretching
  it, or recombining it with another symbol. If you need a variant that
  `brand/` does not contain, ask; a redrawn copy will not match.
- **Suggesting endorsement, affiliation or partnership** that does not exist —
  including "official", "certified" or "in partnership with".

Ask at **bruno@bolalabs.pt**. We are not difficult about this; we just want to
know.

## The reverse case

**RepoManager manages GitHub repositories and is not affiliated with, endorsed
by, or sponsored by GitHub, Inc.** GitHub, the GitHub logo and the Octocat are
trademarks of GitHub, Inc. This project does not use them as its own marks and
neither should a fork — `docs/BRAND.md` lists that as one of the things the
brand system exists to prevent.

## Why a permissive licence and a reserved mark

So that the software travels as far as possible while a user can still tell
whose it is. Anyone may take this code, change it and ship it — that is the
point of Apache-2.0. What they may not do is ship it under our name, because
then "RepoManager" stops telling anyone anything: not whether it is
maintained, not whether it is ours, not whether the security fixes reached it.

The licence protects your freedom to use the code. This page protects a
reader's ability to know what they are looking at.
